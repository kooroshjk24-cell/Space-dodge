# Space Dodge

Fly an Orion-style spacecraft through 11 parts of space - from Low Earth orbit to Deep space.
Dodge the falling walls, aliens, lasers, mines and black holes, shoot from Lunar orbit onwards,
and catch boosters (slow motion, clear skies, shield, rapid fire).

**Play:** https://kooroshjk24-cell.github.io/space-dodge/

On iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap **Share -> Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen
like an app and also works offline.

Controls: the on-screen buttons, or Z / slash to move and comma to shoot on a keyboard.
