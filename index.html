SPACE DODGE

Fly your Orion-style spacecraft through the openings in the falling walls. From any opening you can always reach every opening in the next wall.

  • ◀ ▶ fly left and right (keyboard: Z and /)
  • FIRE shoot, from Lunar orbit (score 30) onwards (keyboard: comma)
  • Catch or shoot glowing capsules for 5 s boosters: slow motion, clear skies, shield, rapid fire
  • Every 30 points: a new part of space, from Low Earth orbit to Deep space
